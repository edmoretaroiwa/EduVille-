Our Story

How EduVille came to be — from a Form 5 dream to a platform for every learner.

💡 The Idea

EduVille started as a simple question asked by a Form 5 student in Chitungwiza: "What if there was a GitHub for education?"

Just like GitHub lets developers share code, EduVille lets students and teachers share past exam papers, notes, and study resources — so no learner ever struggles to find quality material again.

Then came a second thought: "What if there was a TikTok — but only for education?" A place where teachers could stream tutorials, and students could learn in short, engaging videos. That's the video streaming side of EduVille.

🛠️ The Journey

The first attempt to build EduVille was made in Termux (a Linux terminal for Android). It worked while the app was open — but the moment the terminal closed, everything disappeared. It was frustrating, but it proved the idea was worth chasing.

A turning point came when two friends — Edmore Taroiwa and Benjamin Nyamukusa — decided to officially start a project called EduVille. The original name was Eduhubb, but it was renamed to EduVille — a place where education becomes a community.

Today, EduVille is live — hosting courses, video lessons, notes, and a growing community of learners. And this is only the beginning.
